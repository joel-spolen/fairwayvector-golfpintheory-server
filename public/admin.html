What these numbers mean

Accounts, not people. Anonymous accounts are excluded from current accounts, recent sign-ins and known registrations. Registrations include known deleted accounts, but do not identify unique humans. Deletion events can include anonymous or soft-deletion events. Accounts physically deleted before tracking are unknown; a lifetime total cannot be reconstructed.

Logical estimates, not billing. Current-user sync bytes estimate JSONB payload sizes with pg_column_size, including tombstones, history and receipts. Average includes every current account, including accounts with zero sync bytes; maximum is the largest current account’s logical sync estimate.

Different scopes. Physical sync storage includes indexes and all rows in shared sync tables, not just current accounts. Project database storage covers the whole project, not per-user usage. Object storage is unknown, not zero. No iOS/offline local data or shared course cache is counted in the current-user logical sync estimate. These are current measurements, not a future capacity forecast.

Private by design. Only aggregate counts and byte totals are returned. No app user identifiers or individual account records are returned to this dashboard. Metrics are requested from this server only; this browser never connects directly to Supabase.
